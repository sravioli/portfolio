*motions.txt*  Cursor motions in a README

These motions move the cursor in the open README, as in Vim. Most take a
{count}, see |count|. Text is read as rendered, so a line is a line on screen.

==============================================================================
WORDS                                                           *word-motions*

w            Forward to the start of the next word.                      *w*
b            Back to the start of the word.                              *b*
e            Forward to the end of the word.                             *e*
ge           Back to the end of the previous word.                      *ge*
W B E gE     The same for WORDs, which only blanks separate.  *W* *B* *E* *gE*

==============================================================================
LEFT AND RIGHT                                                   *left-right*

0 <Home>     To the first character of the line.                 *0* *<Home>*
^            To the first non-blank character of the line.               *^*
$ <End>      To the end of the line.                              *$* *<End>*
g_           To the last non-blank character of the line.               *g_*
`|`          To column {count}.                                        *bar*
gm           To the middle of the screen width.                         *gm*
gM           To the middle of the line text.                            *gM*
f{char}      To the next {char}.                                         *f*
F{char}      To the previous {char}.                                     *F*
t{char}      Till before the next {char}.                                *t*
T{char}      Till after the previous {char}.                             *T*
;            Repeat the last f, F, t, or T.                              *;*
,            Repeat it in the other direction.                           *,*

As in flash.nvim, f, F, t, and T search past the end of the line, highlight
the characters they can reach next, and repeat when you press the same key
again. See |flash-char|. To jump anywhere on screen, see |flash|.

==============================================================================
UP AND DOWN                                                         *up-down*

+            To the first non-blank of the next line.                    *+*
_            To the first non-blank of the line, {count} minus one lines
             down.                                                       *_*
gj gk        Down or up by display line.                           *gj* *gk*
H            To the top of the screen.                                   *H*
M            To the middle of the screen.                                *M*
L            To the bottom of the screen.                                *L*
{count}%     To {count} percent of the file, as in `50%`.               *N%*

==============================================================================
SCROLLING RELATIVE TO THE CURSOR                                    *scroll-cursor*

zt           Scroll the cursor line to the top of the screen.           *zt*
zz           Scroll the cursor line to the middle.                      *zz*
zb           Scroll the cursor line to the bottom.                      *zb*
z<CR> z. z-  The same as zt, zz, and zb, with the cursor at the first
             non-blank of the line.                         *z<CR>* *z.* *z-*

==============================================================================
TEXT OBJECT MOTIONS                                         *object-motions*

( )          Back or forward a sentence.                           *(* *)*
{ }          Back or forward a paragraph.                          *{* *}*
]] [[        Next or previous heading, see |]]|.
][ []        End of the next or previous section.                *][* *[]*

==============================================================================
BRACKETS                                                       *bracket-motions*

%            To the bracket matching the one under the cursor.           *%*
[( [{        Back to the unmatched ( or {.                         *[(* *[{*
]) ]}        Forward to the unmatched ) or }.                      *])* *]}*

==============================================================================
MARKS                                                                  *marks*

m{a-z}       Set a mark at the cursor.                                   *m*
m{A-Z}       Set a mark that works across READMEs.                      *mA*
'{a-z}       Jump to the line of the mark.                               *'*
`{a-z}       Jump to the exact spot of the mark.                         *`*
''           Jump back to where you were before the last jump.          *''*
